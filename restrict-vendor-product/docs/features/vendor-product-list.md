# Vendor Product List

Vendors can easily track the status of all their product requests from their dashboard.

By navigating to their **Product List**, vendors will see an overview of their items along with the current approval status assigned by the store admin.

![Vendor Product List](/images/webkul-magento2-restrict-vendor-product-vendor-product-list-with-status.webp)

The status column will clearly indicate whether a product request is Pending, Approved, or Rejected. This transparency helps vendors manage their catalog effectively and understand which products are live on the store.

## Edit Product Attributes

From the product list, the seller can also edit their products. When editing, the seller can modify the product attributes, but they are limited to changing only the attributes that are allowed by the admin in the module's configuration.

![Vendor Edit Product](/images/webkul-magento2-restrict-vendor-product-vendor-edit-product.png)
