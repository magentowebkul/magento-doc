# Product Approval Email

When the store admin takes action on a product request, the module automatically trigger an email notification to the vendor.

If the product is approved or disapproved, the vendor receives an email notifying the status of their product.

![Product Approval Email](/images/webkul-magento2-restrict-vendor-product-product-approval-email.webp)

This automated email communication ensures that vendors are kept up-to-date in real-time, reducing the need for manual follow-up and improving the overall seller experience on the marketplace.

## Email Notification Triggers

The system is designed to keep communication flowing smoothly without manual intervention. Emails are triggered in the following scenarios:

- **Product Approved**: The vendor receives an email letting them know their product request was approved by the admin.
- **Product Disapproved**: The vendor receives an alert stating that their product request was rejected by the admin.

## Customizing the Email Templates

To maintain brand consistency, the store owner can fully customize these email notifications:

1. **Template Location**: Navigate to the marketing and communications section in the Magento admin panel to find the specific email templates for this module.
2. **Dynamic Variables**: The templates support dynamic variables, meaning you can automatically include the vendor's name, the specific product name, and the updated status directly in the email body.
3. **Branding**: Admins can update the logo, colors, and typography of the emails to match the storefront's visual identity.
