# Product Approval Email

When the store admin takes action on a product request, the module automatically trigger an email notification to the vendor.

If the product is approved or disapproved, the vendor receives an email notifying the status of their product.

![Product Approval Email](/images/webkul-magento2-restrict-vendor-product-product-approval-email.webp)

This automated email communication ensures that vendors are kept up-to-date in real-time, reducing the need for manual follow-up and improving the overall seller experience on the marketplace.
