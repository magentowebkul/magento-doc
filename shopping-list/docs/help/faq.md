# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Which product types are supported by the Shopping List module?
The module fully supports all Magento core product types:
- Simple Products
- Configurable Products
- Downloadable Products
- Grouped Products
- Bundle Products

---

### 2. Can guest customers create shopping lists?
No, shopping list management requires an authenticated customer account. Guest users will be prompted to log in or register before creating shopping lists.

---

### 3. Can a customer create multiple shopping lists?
Yes, registered customers can create an unlimited number of custom-named shopping lists (e.g., *Personal*, *Work*, *Weekly Grocery*).

---

### 4. Is the module compatible with Hyvä Theme?
Yes, the backend business logic operates independently of the storefront theme layer. For Hyvä themes, custom Alpine.js / Tailwind templates can interface directly with the shopping list endpoints.

---

### 5. Where can I get support or request custom enhancements?
For support or custom feature requests, submit a ticket at [Webkul Support Desk](https://support.uvdesk.com/en/).
