# Troubleshooting Guide

Find quick solutions to common setup, configuration, and runtime issues encountered with **Magento 2 Multi Vendor Recurring Payments & Subscription**.

---

## 1. "No Payment method available" at Checkout

### Symptom
When a customer attempts to check out with a subscription item, the payment step displays:
```text
"No Payment method available."
```

### Cause
The administrator has not explicitly selected allowed payment methods for subscriptions in store configuration. Standard Magento payment methods are hidden for subscription quotes by default.

### Resolution
1. Go to **Stores > Configuration > Subscription Configuration > General Settings**.
2. Locate the field **Allowed payment methods to buy subscription**.
3. Hold `Ctrl` (or `Cmd`) and select the recurring methods you want to offer:
   * `Webkul Recurring Stripe Payment`
   * `Webkul Recurring PayPal Express Checkout`
   * Or offline methods (e.g. Check / Money order)
4. Click **Save Config** and run:
   ```bash
   php bin/magento cache:flush
   ```

## 2. Renewal Child Orders Not Appearing Under Subscription

### Symptom
The initial order was placed and shows under *Subscription Information*, but the *Orders* tab remains empty over time.

### Cause
The *Orders* tab in the subscription detail view only tracks subsequent renewal orders generated by future billing cycles, not the initial purchase order. If renewal cycles have elapsed without new orders:
1. Magento cron jobs are not running.
2. Gateway webhooks (Stripe webhook or PayPal IPN) are failing to reach your server.

### Resolution
1. Verify Magento cron is active:
   ```bash
   crontab -l
   # Ensure: * * * * * /usr/bin/php /path/to/magento/bin/magento cron:run
   ```
2. Verify webhook URLs in Stripe and PayPal dashboards:
   * **Stripe**: `https://[your-domain]/recurring/stripe/notify/`
   * **PayPal**: `https://[your-domain]/recurring/paypal/notify`
3. Verify that the endpoints respond with HTTP 200 and that your domain has a valid SSL certificate.

## 3. "You can not add other product with subscription product" Error

### Symptom
Customer sees an error banner when trying to add a regular item while a subscription product is in the cart.

### Cause
Recurring billing engines cannot mix one-time purchase items and subscription items in the same checkout transaction because payment gateways tokenize recurring agreements specifically for the recurring schedule.

### Resolution
Advise the customer to complete the subscription purchase first and then place a separate order for standard one-time catalog products.

---

## 4. "Start dates and Duration types of all added subscriptions must be same"

### Symptom
Customer receives an error when adding a second subscription product to the cart.

### Cause
Multiple subscription items can only be billed together if they share the identical **Duration Type** (e.g. Monthly) and the exact same **Start Date**.

### Resolution
Ensure the customer selects matching start dates and frequencies for all subscription products added to the shopping cart.

## 5. Subscription Form Not Displaying on Hyvä Theme

### Symptom
The subscription plan dropdown and "Subscribe" button do not render on product pages when running the Hyvä theme.

### Cause
Standard Luma XML blocks are not automatically rendered in Hyvä without Hyvä-compatible view models and templates.

### Resolution
Ensure that `Webkul_RecurringHyva` is installed and that the product view layout contains the proper `view_model` declaration:
```xml
<arguments>
    <argument name="view_model" xsi:type="object">Webkul\Recurring\ViewModel\Recurring</argument>
    <argument name="mp_view_model" xsi:type="object">Webkul\MpRecurringPayment\ViewModel\Recurring</argument>
</arguments>
```
Compile and flush cache:
```bash
php bin/magento setup:di:compile
php bin/magento cache:flush
```
