# Subscription Reports & Analytics

The **Subscription Report** gives administrators deep insight into recurring revenue, subscriber retention, and catalog performance through a centralized analytics dashboard.

---

## Accessing Subscription Reports

1. In the Magento Admin Panel, navigate to **Recurring > Subscription Reports** (or **Reports** under the Recurring menu).
2. The interactive subscription performance dashboard will display.

![Subscription Report Overview](/images/report-1.webp)

---

## Executive KPI Cards

At the top of the report, key performance indicators provide an instant snapshot of your subscription health:

* **Subscription Revenue**: Total revenue generated from subscription initial fees and recurring installment cycles.
* **Active Subscriptions**: Total count of recurring profiles currently active and generating renewals.
* **Average Subscription Value (ASV)**: Average revenue earned per active subscription across billing periods.
* **New Subscriptions**: Count of newly acquired subscribers within the selected filtering time window.

---

## Date Range & Website Filtering

Admins can tailor report metrics using intuitive filtering parameters:

* **Date Range**: Filter data by *Today*, *Current Month*, *Last 30 Days*, *Current Year*, or a customized date interval.
* **Website / Store View**: Scope reporting to specific multi-tenant websites or localized store views.

---

## Visual Analytics & Churn Analysis

Interactive visual charts map recurring revenue progression over time:

![Subscription Graphical Charts & Breakdown](/images/lastt.webp)

* **Revenue Progression Chart**: Visualizes cash flow generated per cycle to track subscription revenue growth.
* **Churn Rate Analysis**: Highlights the percentage of subscriptions cancelled or expired to help optimize retention strategies.

---

## Detailed Product-Wise Breakdown Grid

Below the trend charts, a granular grid breaks down performance per individual subscription product:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Product Name** | Catalog product offered on subscription. |
| **Subscription Type** | Duration frequency attached to the product. |
| **Product Revenue** | Cumulative revenue generated by this product's subscriptions. |
| **Active Subscriptions** | Number of active paying customers for this specific product. |
| **Subscription Status** | Current status breakdown (`Active`, `Paused`, `Cancelled`). |
