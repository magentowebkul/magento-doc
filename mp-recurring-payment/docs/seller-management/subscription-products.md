# Seller Subscription Products

Marketplace sellers can create new subscription products or convert existing catalog items into recurring subscription services from their vendor dashboard.

---

## Part 1: Creating a New Subscription Product

1. In the seller dashboard, navigate to **My Account > New Products**.
2. Select the product type (**Simple**, **Configurable**, **Virtual**, or **Downloadable**) and click **Continue**.

![Seller Add New Product](/images/webkul-magento2-multi-vendor-recurring-payments-add-new-product-seller-end.png)

3. Fill in the standard product parameters (Product Name, SKU, Description, Category, Base Price, Stock Quantity).
4. Scroll to the bottom of the form to find the **Subscription Configuration** section.
5. Set the **Subscription** field to **Yes**.

![Seller Subscription Configuration Section](/images/webkul-magento2-multi-vendor-recurring-payments-subscription-configuration-section-seller-end-for-new-poduct.png)

6. Click **Edit Configurations** to open the subscription plan configuration interface.

![Seller Add Subscription Types](/images/webkul-magento2-multi-vendor-recurring-payments-seller-end-add-subscription-types-for-new-poduct.png)

7. Configure your subscription plans:
   * Select one or more **Duration Types** (e.g. Weekly, Monthly, Yearly).
   * Specify the **Initial Fee** (one-time setup fee charged immediately upon checkout).
   * Specify the **Subscription Amount** (recurring installment charged each renewal cycle).
8. Click **Save** to publish the product.

---

## Part 2: Converting an Existing Product

1. Navigate to **My Account > My Product List** in the seller dashboard.
2. Select any non-subscription product and click **Edit**.

![Seller Edit Existing Product](/images/webkul-magento2-multi-vendor-recurring-payments-edit-seller-product.png)

3. Scroll to the bottom of the page to locate **Subscription Configuration**.
4. Set **Subscription** to **Yes**.

![Enable Subscription on Existing Item](/images/webkul-magento2-multi-vendor-recurring-payments-subscription-configuration-section-seller-end-for-existing-product.png)

5. Click **Edit Configurations** to add desired subscription terms and pricing.

![Added Subscription Types to Existing Product](/images/webkul-magento2-multi-vendor-recurring-payments-seller-end-added-subscription-types-for-existing-poduct.png)

6. Click **Save** to update the product.

The product will now feature subscription plans and recurring billing options on the customer storefront.
