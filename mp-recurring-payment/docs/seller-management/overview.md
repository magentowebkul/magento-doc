# Seller Workflow Overview

Marketplace vendors have complete autonomy to offer subscription-based products, configure flexible billing plans, connect their payout accounts, and manage their subscribers directly from their frontend vendor dashboard.

---

## Seller Navigation Menu

When logged in to their marketplace vendor account, sellers navigate to the **Recurring Subscriptions** section in the account sidebar:

![Seller Recurring Subscriptions Menu](/images/webkul-magento2-multi-vendor-recurring-payments-seller-end-option.png)

Hovering over or clicking **Recurring Subscriptions** presents three dedicated sub-menus:

1. **Manage Product Subscriptions**: View and manage all customers subscribed to the seller's products, monitor renewal states, view profile IDs, and process cancellations.
2. **Connect To Stripe**: Complete Stripe Connect onboarding to receive direct payouts or authorize customer subscription billing.
3. **Manage PayPal Details**: Provide verified PayPal merchant information and recipient email address for automated PayPal Mass Payment disbursements.

---

## Seller Capabilities Matrix

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Product Conversion** | Enable subscription on simple, configurable, virtual, and downloadable products. |
| **Custom Plan Pricing** | Define custom initial fee and recurring installment amount for each duration. |
| **Payment Onboarding** | Connect Stripe and set up PayPal details independently. |
| **Subscriber Management** | Review customer details, start dates, and subscription status. |
| **Seller Cancellation** | Cancel/unsubscribe a customer profile if needed. |
