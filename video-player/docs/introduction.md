# Introduction

**Product Video for Magento 2 (Webkul_VideoPlayer)** extension empowers store owners and administrators to upload product videos directly and embed streaming media (YouTube, Vimeo, Google Drive, and CDN) onto product detail pages.

Adding rich video demonstrations to product pages significantly enhances customer engagement, reduces return rates, and drives conversions by giving shoppers a clear, multi-dimensional view of products in action. The extension works seamlessly across all Magento product types: **Simple Product**, **Configurable Product**, **Virtual Product**, **Grouped Product**, **Bundle Product**, and **Downloadable Product**.

---

## Key Features

- **Expanded Video Format Support:** Upload product videos in a wide variety of formats: **MP4**, **WebM**, **MOV**, **WMV**, **FLV**, **MKV**, and **3GP**, with customizable preview thumbnail images.
- **Multiple Video Sources:** Supports uploaded media files as well as third-party video links from **YouTube**, **Vimeo**, **Google Drive**, and custom **CDN URLs**.
- **Custom Video Player Appearance:** Brand the video player by customizing the **Primary Color** and **Text/Icon Color** to harmonize with your store theme.
- **Interactive Player Controls:**
  - Center controls overlay on pause: **Skip Backward 10 Seconds**, **Play/Pause**, and **Skip Forward 10 Seconds**.
  - Bottom control bar: Play/Pause, scrubber progress bar, playback speed options (0.5x to 2x), Picture-in-Picture (PiP), mute/volume slider, and fullscreen mode.
- **Timeline Chapter Markers:** Add timestamps (in `mm:ss` or `hh:mm:ss` format) with chapter titles, creating clickable navigation markers on the player's progress bar.
- **Direct Video Downloads:** Optionally enable a download button allowing customers to download individual product video files directly from the player.
- **Video Info Overlay:** Built-in information button overlay showcasing the video title and detailed product description.
- **Mass Product Video Upload:** Bulk upload videos and feature images mapped by product SKU using a simple CSV file and ZIP archives.
- **Hyvä Theme Compatibility:** Fully compatible with modern Hyvä storefronts, delivering blazingly fast load times and clean, responsive UI layouts.
- **Multi-Language & RTL Ready:** Supports complete localization via standard Magento translation dictionaries (`i18n`), including RTL (Right-to-Left) and LTR (Left-to-Right) layouts.
