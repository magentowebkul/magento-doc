# Product Edit & Storefront Display

Once products are mass uploaded, linked relationships (related products, up-sells, cross-sells) and product information are immediately active in both the product edit panel and customer storefront.

---

## Product Edit View (Admin & Seller Panel)

When editing a mass-uploaded product via **Catalog > Products > Edit Product**:

1. All basic attributes, categories, and images specified in the upload file are pre-populated.
2. Under the **Related Products, Up-Sells, and Cross-Sells** tab, administrators and sellers can view or modify the linked products imported via CSV/XLS/XML.

![Product Edit Page - Related Products View](/images/image-59.png)
![Product Edit Page - Cross-Sell and Up-Sell Linkage](/images/image-60.png)

## Storefront Front-End View

Customers browsing the online store can view mass-uploaded products with full image galleries, prices, descriptions, and variant swatches.

![Storefront Catalog Product Page View](/images/image-61.png)

Uploaded **Related** and **Up-Sell** items appear directly on the product detail page under the main description:
