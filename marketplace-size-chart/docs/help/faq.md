# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find quick answers to common questions about the **Webkul Magento 2 Marketplace Size Chart Template** extension.

---

### Can marketplace sellers create their own unique size units and templates?
**Yes.** Marketplace sellers have their own dedicated **Marketplace Size Chart** menu in their seller dashboard. They can create brand-specific size units, custom measurements with guide images, and multi-dimensional size chart templates independently from the store administrator.

---

### Does the extension support configurable products?
**Yes.** The extension supports both **Simple** and **Configurable** products. Assigning a size chart template to a configurable parent product displays the interactive size chart modal on the storefront product detail page.

---

### How does the Custom Size Chart feature work for buyers?
When enabled, shoppers can click the **Custom Size Chart** tab in the popup modal. They are guided step-by-step with visual diagrams showing how to measure each body part (e.g., Chest, Waist, Inseam). Customers enter their measurements, select their body type (*Plump*, *Normal*, or *Sportive*), accept the disclaimer, and save the measurements directly to their cart.

---

### Where can store owners and sellers view customer custom measurements?
- **Store Administrators:** In **Sales > Orders > View Order**, the custom dimensions and body type appear in the ordered item's options section.
- **Marketplace Sellers:** In **Marketplace > My Order History > View Order**, sellers can inspect the submitted dimensions for fulfilling custom tailoring or bespoke apparel.
- **Customers:** In **My Account > My Orders**, buyers can review their submitted custom measurements.

---

### Can the disclaimer and footer instructions be customized?
**Yes.** Navigate to **Stores > Configuration > Webkul > Marketplace Size Chart > General Settings > Custom Chart Configuration**. You can customize the **Acknowledgement Note** and **Footer Note** text for each store view.

---

### Is the extension compatible with the Webkul Marketplace Seller Group module?
**Yes.** The extension checks for the presence of the Seller Group module and enforces permission-based access rules for Size Units, Measurements, and Templates in the seller dashboard navigation.
