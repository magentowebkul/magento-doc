# Managing Wishlists

The **Magento 2 Multi Wishlist** module gives registered customers and guest shoppers full control over organizing saved products into multiple named custom wishlists.

---

## Customer Dashboard Overview

When a customer logs in and navigates to **My Account > Manage Wish List**, they are presented with an enhanced wishlist interface featuring both custom wishlists and the built-in **Default** wishlist:

![Wishlist Toolbar Actions](/images/wishlist_toolbar.png)

* **Wishlist Navigation:** View saved items organized under individual wishlist sections.
* **Wishlist Toolbar Actions:** Perform actions per wishlist, including **Update Wish List**, **Move All to Wish List**, **Share Wish List**, **Add All to Cart**, **Rename Wish List**, and **Delete Wish List**.

---

## Creating a New Wishlist

1. **From Product or Category Pages:**
   - Click **Add to Wish List**.
   - In the popup, select an existing wishlist or enter a new wishlist name (e.g., *Summer Holiday Essentials*).
   - Click **Add To Wish List**. The item is added directly to the selected or newly created wishlist.

![Create New Wishlist on Product or Category Pages](/images/create_wishlist_on_product_or_category_page.png)

2. **From Customer Account Dashboard:**
   - Inside **My Account > Manage Wish List**, click the **Create New Wish List** button.
   - Enter the wishlist name and confirm to create a new wishlist container.

![Create New Wishlist](/images/create_new_wishlist.png)

---

## Renaming & Deleting Custom Wishlists

* **Rename Wish List:** Click **Rename Wish List** next to any custom wishlist, enter the updated name, and save. *(Note: The system **Default** wishlist cannot be renamed).*

![Rename Wishlist](/images/rename_wishlist.png)

* **Delete Wish List:** Click **Delete Wish List** on a custom wishlist and confirm. Deleting a custom wishlist removes the wishlist entry. *(Note: The system **Default** wishlist cannot be deleted).*

![Delete Wishlist](/images/delete_wishlist.png)

---

## Managing & Moving Wishlist Items

Customers can update, transfer, or clear items across wishlists:

* **Move All Items:** Click **Move All to Wish List** on a wishlist block to bulk-transfer all saved items into another custom or default wishlist.
* **Update Item Quantities:** Modify item quantities or options and click **Update Wish List** to save changes.
* **Add All to Cart:** Click **Add All to Cart** to transfer all available items in that wishlist directly into the active shopping cart.
* **Remove Item:** Delete individual products from any wishlist using the item delete action.
