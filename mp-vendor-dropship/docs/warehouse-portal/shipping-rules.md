# Warehouse Shipping Rules Management

Warehouse managers can maintain their own localized table rate shipping rules directly from their portal, ensuring courier fees align with their dispatch agreements and regional freight costs.

---

## Accessing Warehouse Shipping Rules

In the warehouse portal sidebar, click on **Manage Shipping Rule** under **Vendor Dropship Menu**.

![Warehouse Shipping Rules Grid](/images/shipping-rules-grid.png)

---

## Adding Warehouse Shipping Rates

To define new table rates for this warehouse:

1. Click **Add New Shipping Rules**.
2. The import rate modal opens:

![Import Shipping Rules Section](/images/shipping-rules-import.png)

3. Download the sample CSV file.
4. Fill in destination criteria (Country, State, Zip range, Weight limits, and Rate).
5. Select the CSV file via **Browse** and click **Save**.

The newly uploaded shipping matrix immediately takes effect for customer orders fulfilled through this warehouse.

---

## Removing Obsolete Rules

Warehouse managers can remove expired or outdated courier rates:
1. Select the rules to remove in the grid.
2. Select **Delete** from the Actions menu.
3. Confirm deletion to update the shipping table.
