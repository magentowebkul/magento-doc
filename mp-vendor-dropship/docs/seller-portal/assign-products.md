# Seller Product Warehouse Assignment

Marketplace sellers can distribute their products across multiple warehouses directly from their frontend vendor dashboard, enabling multi-location inventory fulfillment for their catalog.

---

## Accessing Seller Product List

1. The seller logs in to their customer account on the storefront.
2. From the vendor dashboard sidebar, navigate to **Marketplace > My Product List**.

![Seller Product List](/images/vendor-Product-List.png)

---

## Assigning Warehouses & Custom Stock Quantities

To link a product to one or more warehouses:

1. In the seller's product listing, find the product and click **Edit**.
2. Scroll to the **Assign Product to Warehouses** section.

![Seller Assign Product to Warehouses](/images/vendor-Product-List-1.png)

3. For each available warehouse:
   - Check the checkbox next to the warehouse name to enable stock storage at that facility.
   - Specify the available **Quantity** stored in that warehouse.
4. Click **Save Product**.

::: tip Multi-Warehouse Stock Splitting
A seller can allocate 50 units to a New York warehouse and 30 units to a Los Angeles warehouse. When a customer orders, the system automatically checks stock and fulfills from the closest warehouse holding adequate inventory.
:::
