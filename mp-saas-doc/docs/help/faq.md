# Frequently Asked Questions & Support

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can vendors upgrade or switch their membership plans?
Yes. When a vendor purchases a new duration plan, the new tier overrides the existing subscription schedule upon successful authorization, updating their billing cycle and tenant parameters.

### What happens when a subscription expires or fails to renew?
If a vendor's subscription expires or automated billing fails, their membership status transitions to **Expired**. Once expired, standard marketplace commission deductions automatically resume for all subsequent orders until the subscription is renewed.

### Can the marketplace administrator assign free or complimentary subscriptions?
Yes. Administrators can navigate to **Marketplace Management > Manage Seller**, select the target vendors, and use the mass action **Assign a Membership Plan** to grant membership privileges directly without requiring payment gateway checkout.

### Is Hyvä Theme supported?
Yes. The extension offers full compatibility with Hyvä frontend themes through the dedicated companion package `Webkul_MpSaasHyva`.

### Can vendors retain their existing custom domains (e.g., `brand.com` instead of a subdomain)?
The base module provisions wildcard subdomains (e.g., `vendor.marketplace.com`). For fully custom root domain mapping, please consult Webkul support for custom enterprise routing integrations.
