# Customer Portal & Front-End

Guide to the storefront price drop alert subscription experience for guest visitors and registered store customers.

### Subscribing on Simple Products

1. Open any product page. The **Price Drop Alert** subscription box appears below the **Add to Cart** button.
2. Enter your email address and click **Subscribe**.

![Front End Subscribe](/images/front-subscribe.webp)

---

### Subscribing on Configurable Products

For configurable products with options (e.g. Size, Color), select your options before subscribing:

### Customer Account - Subscribed Product List

Registered customers can manage subscriptions in their account dashboard:

1. Log into customer account.
2. Navigate to **Subscribed Product List** in the left menu.

![Subscribed Product List](/images/subscription-list.webp)

- Click **Unsubscribe** on any item. A success notice will confirm removal:

![Unsubscribe Notice](/images/unsubscribe-notice.webp)

- **Bulk Unsubscribe**: Select multiple checkboxes and click **Unsubscribe** to remove subscriptions in bulk.

![Bulk Unsubscribe](/images/multiple-unsubscribe.webp)
