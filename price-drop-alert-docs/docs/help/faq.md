# Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Magento product types are supported?
The extension supports **Simple**, **Virtual**, **Downloadable**, and **Configurable** products.

---

### Can guest (non-registered) visitors subscribe to price drop alerts?
Yes. Guests can enter their email address to subscribe. They receive confirmation and price drop emails containing direct unsubscription links.

---

### Is there any limit on how many products a customer can subscribe to?
No. Registered customers and guest users can subscribe to an unlimited number of products.

---

### Is the extension compatible with Hyvä Theme?
Yes. Magento 2 Price Drop Alert is fully compatible with Hyvä Theme and includes GraphQL API support.

---

### How do registered customers manage their subscriptions?
Registered customers can log into their account, navigate to **Subscribed Product List**, and view or bulk-unsubscribe from alert subscriptions.
