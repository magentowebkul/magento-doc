# Seller Product Export

Marketplace sellers can export their existing catalog items into CSV files for backup, offline editing, or cross-platform inventory management.

---

## Exporting Seller Products

1. In the seller panel, go to **Marketplace > Mass Upload Product Export**.

![Mass Upload Product Export Menu Link](/images/image-40.png)

2. Select the **Product Type** to export (Simple, Configurable, Virtual, or Downloadable).
3. Select any **Custom Attributes** you wish to include as columns in the exported file.

![Select Product Type and Custom Attributes for Export](/images/image-41.png)

4. Click **Export**. The browser will download a generated `.csv` file containing the selected product data and attributes.

![Exported Product CSV File](/images/image-42.png)
