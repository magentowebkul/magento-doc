# Introduction

**Magento 2 Recurring Plan For Discount** module helps store owners give discounts through simple, automated subscription plans.

Store administrators can create weekly, monthly, or yearly recurring discount plans for any product in their catalog. In addition, the extension supports online recurring billing through **Stripe** and **PayPal Express Checkout**, ensuring recurring renewal payments are handled securely and automatically.

::: tip
The module allows store owners to build customer loyalty and drive repeat purchases by offering attractive discount subscriptions that automatically apply discounts during checkout.
:::

## Features

- **Flexible Subscription Cycles:** Create weekly, monthly, or yearly plans based on your store's commercial strategy.
- **Multiple Discount Types:** Offer fixed amount or percentage-based discounts to subscribed shoppers.
- **Automatic Cart Discounts:** Subscribed customers receive automatic discounts applied directly to their cart when products are added.
- **Integrated Payment Gateways:** Support for online recurring payments with PayPal Express Checkout and Stripe.
- **Customer Subscription Dashboard:** Customers can view, manage, and cancel their subscribed plans directly under their account dashboard.
- **Backend Subscription Management:** Store administrators can view and manage all active, pending, and expired customer subscriptions from a centralized admin grid.
- **Automated Email Notifications:** Send automated notification emails when a subscription starts, renews, nears expiration, or cancels.
- **Toggle Anytime:** Turn recurring discount features on or off as required from the system configuration.
