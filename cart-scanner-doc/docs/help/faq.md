# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions and answers regarding the Shopping Cart Scanner module.

### Q: Does the module support both Guest and Registered Users?
**A:** Yes! Guest shoppers and registered customers can both use QR scanning and cart sharing seamlessly without registration barriers.

### Q: What happens to existing items in the mobile cart upon scanning?
**A:** You can configure the **Cart Sync Strategy** under General Settings to either **Merge** scanned items with the current mobile cart or **Replace** the mobile cart completely.

### Q: Is Hyvä Theme supported?
**A:** Yes. The extension includes native Alpine.js and Tailwind CSS compatibility via the `Webkul_CartScannerHyva` package.

### Q: Where can admins track scan analytics and sales revenue?
**A:** Navigate to **Cart Scanner > Scan Analytics & Reports** in the Magento Admin backend to view real-time KPI stat cards and interactive UI Grid logs.

### Q: How can I contact Webkul support for custom requirements?
**A:** You can submit a support ticket at [Webkul UVDesk Portal](https://webkul.uvdesk.com/) or visit the official [Webkul Store](https://store.webkul.com/).
