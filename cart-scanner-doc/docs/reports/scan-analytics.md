# Admin Scan Analytics & Conversion Reports

Store administrators can track the performance and ROI of mobile QR scanning by navigating to **Cart Scanner > Scan Analytics & Reports**:

![Admin Menu Navigation](/images/image-10.png)

The dashboard provides real-time KPI overview metric cards and an interactive UI Grid:

![Scan Analytics Dashboard](/images/image-11.png)

## Key Metrics Tracked

1. **Total QR Scans:** Total number of times desktop QR codes were scanned.
2. **Converted Orders:** Total orders successfully placed on mobile after a QR scan.
3. **Conversion Rate (%):** Percentage ratio of converted orders to total scans.
4. **Generated Revenue ($):** Total sales revenue generated from QR scan conversions.
5. **Interactive UI Grid:** Displays detailed records including Scanned Date, Converted Date, Source Cart ID, Mobile Cart ID, Device Type (iOS/Android/Desktop), Channel, Conversion Status (SCANNED, CONVERTED, CANCELLED, REFUNDED), Order Number, Order Revenue, and Client IP.
