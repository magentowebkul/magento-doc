# Mobile In-App Scanner & Mobile Mini-Cart QR Widget

When customers browse the storefront on a mobile device, two powerful cross-device shopping tools are available directly in the mobile header:

* **Camera Scanner Icon `[▪]`:** Positioned in the top navigation bar between search and the cart icon. Shoppers can tap this icon to activate the mobile camera scanner, point it at the desktop screen, and instantly transfer and synchronize all products into their mobile cart.
* **Mobile Mini-Cart QR Code Widget:** When the shopper taps the cart icon to open the Mini-Cart dropdown on mobile, the compact **"Scan & Buy on Mobile"** QR code widget with a live countdown timer and *"Click to enlarge"* option is also rendered. This enables seamless cart sharing with friends/family or transferring the cart to another mobile device on the fly.
