# Export Orders

## Export Magento Orders To Shopify
Export Magento 2 orders directly to Shopify in a few simple steps. The connector supports exporting different order statuses such as Complete, Processing, Closed, Pending, Refunded, etc.

1. Go to **Webkul Shopify Connector -> Export Orders To Shopify**.
2. Select the required orders.
3. Choose **Export To Shopify** from the action dropdown.
4. Click **Submit**.
5. This navigates you to the "Run Profile" page where the execution occurs.

![Export Orders Grid](/images/magento2-order-export-to-shopify.webp)


![Order Export Profiler](/images/magento2-profiler-run-to-export-orders.webp)

Once the export profile runs successfully, the orders become visible in the Shopify admin panel under the **Orders** section.
