# Introduction

## Overview

The **Magento 2 Parcel Perfect Shipping (Webkul_ParcelPerfectShipping)** extension integrates Magento 2 online stores with **Parcel Perfect**, a courier and freight software management solution providing parcel tracking technology and real-time shipping rate calculations across Southern Africa, the UK, Europe, USA, Australia, Singapore, Hong Kong, and mainland China.

By installing this shipping module, store administrators can calculate live shipping rates at cart and checkout, generate dynamic Parcel Perfect shipping labels in PDF format, configure handling fees, manage packaging dimensions, and dispatch automated parcel tracking details directly to customers via email.

---

## Core Capabilities

- **Real-Time Freight Rate Calculation:** Dynamically calculates shipping costs based on origin, destination, and package characteristics using Parcel Perfect web services.
- **Global & Regional Coverage:** Serves domestic courier services across Southern Africa as well as international destinations including the UK, Europe, USA, Australia, Singapore, Hong Kong, and mainland China.
- **Development & Live Sandbox Mode:** Easily toggle between development sandbox and live production web services for rigorous testing.
- **Handling Fee Management:** Apply custom handling surcharges on top of standard courier shipping charges.
- **Storefront Shipping Estimator:** Enables customers to estimate Parcel Perfect shipping rates directly on the Shopping Cart page by specifying postal/zip code.
- **Seamless Checkout Integration:** Displays available Parcel Perfect shipping methods clearly on the checkout shipping step.
- **Order & Invoice Administration:** Generates invoices containing complete shipping information and courier costs.
- **Custom Package Packaging:** Configure package dimensions (length, width, height) and total package weights before booking shipments.
- **Automated PDF Shipping Labels:** Print and download professional Parcel Perfect shipping labels with barcodes directly from the Magento admin order view.
- **Automated Tracking Notifications:** Tracking numbers are generated upon shipment creation and can be dispatched to customers with one click via email.
- **Multi-Lingual & Translatable:** Full compatibility with Magento 2 locale configurations and standard `i18n` translation dictionaries.

---

## Key Benefits

- **Accurate Courier Pricing:** Eliminates undercharging and manual freight quotation by fetching live rates directly from Parcel Perfect.
- **End-to-End Shipment Lifecycle:** Manages the full fulfillment journey: rate calculation, invoice generation, package dimension setup, label printing, and customer tracking.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Print barcode-ready shipping labels directly from Magento admin without leaving the backend.
- **Broad Geographic Reach:** Optimized for Southern African courier networks and major global commerce hubs.
- **Open & Extensible Code:** Clean, modular Magento 2 architecture compliant with best coding standards.
