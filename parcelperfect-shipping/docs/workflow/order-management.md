# Order & Invoice Management

Store administrators can manage orders placed with Parcel Perfect Shipping, verify courier rates, and generate and print invoices containing shipping breakdowns.

---

## 1. Viewing Customer Orders

Once an order is submitted on the storefront:

1. Log in to the Magento Admin Panel.
2. Navigate to **Sales > Orders**.
3. In the orders grid, locate the relevant order and click **View** under the **Action** column.

![Admin Orders Grid](/images/Check-order-details-1200x586.png)

---

## 2. Reviewing Shipping & Payment Details

Inside the Order View screen, administrators can inspect comprehensive details:
- **Payment & Shipping Method:** Shows the exact Parcel Perfect shipping method selected by the customer along with the total shipping and handling charge.
- **Shipping and Handling Fee Breakdown:** Displays base shipping cost plus any configured surcharge.
- **Order Action Controls:** Buttons to generate invoices, ship items, re-order, send order emails, or cancel the order.

![Order Payment and Shipping Information](/images/Payment-and-shipping-information-1200x635.png)

---

## 3. Creating Invoices

To create an invoice for the order:

1. In the upper action bar of the order page, click **Invoice**.
2. Review the invoice items and shipping amounts. The invoice reflects all shipping details and fees calculated for that specific order.
3. Click **Submit Invoice**.

![Invoice Details View](/images/invoice-details-1156x1024.png)

---

## 4. Printing Invoices

Store administrators can print hard-copy or PDF invoices directly from the admin panel:

1. Inside the generated invoice view, click the **Print** button in the top action bar.

![Click on Print Invoice Button](/images/click-on-print-1200x630.png)

2. A print-ready PDF invoice is generated containing order items, billing/shipping addresses, and Parcel Perfect shipping charges.

![Printed Invoice PDF Preview](/images/Print-invoice.png)
