# Frontend Module Workflow

Customers can subscribe to newsletters directly from the storefront.

---

## Direct Email Subscription

1. Enter an email address in the storefront footer subscription field.
2. Click the **Subscribe** button.
3. The email is added to the subscriber list, and newsletters or promotional emails are sent based on configured templates.

![Direct Email Subscription](/images/frontend/01.webp)

---

## Newsletter Sign-Up During Account Creation

Customers can subscribe to newsletters while creating a new account. On the registration page, a **Sign Up for Newsletter** checkbox is available. When selected, the customer’s email is automatically added to the newsletter subscription list after account creation.

![Newsletter Sign-Up During Registration](/images/frontend/02.webp)

---

## Manage Newsletter Subscription

Customers can manage their newsletter preferences from the **My Account** &rarr; **Newsletter Subscriptions** section:

1. Select or deselect the **General Subscription** option.
2. Click the **Save** button to update the subscription status immediately.

![Manage Newsletter Subscription](/images/frontend/03.webp)
