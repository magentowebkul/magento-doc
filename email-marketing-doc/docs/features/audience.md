# Audience

The admin will navigate through **Email Marketing** &rarr; **Audience**.

---

## Audience List

Here, we can see the customers who subscribe to Emails.

![Audience List](/images/audience/01.webp)

The admin can edit the details of the customer by clicking on the **Edit** button under the **Action** column.

![Edit Customer Newsletter](/images/audience/02.webp)

---

## Newsletter

Here the admin can check Newsletter information such as:

* **Subscribed to Newsletter**
* **Subscribed on store view**
* **Last Date Subscribed**
* **Email Marketing Tags**
