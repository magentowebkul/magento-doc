# Customer Tagging

The **Magento 2 Email Marketing** module lets you create custom **Customer Tags** and assign them to customers for targeted marketing campaigns.

---

## Managing Customer Tags

To manage customer tags, navigate to **Email Marketing** &rarr; **Customer Tag** in the Admin panel.

![Customer Tag Grid](/images/customer-tag/01.webp)

### Adding a New Customer Tag
1. Click the **Add Tag** button in the top right.
2. Enter the **Tag Label** (e.g., `VIP`, `Frequent Buyers`, `Cart Abandoners`, `Newsletter Subscribers`).
3. Click **Save Tag**.

![Add New Tag](/images/customer-tag/02.webp)

### Editing or Deleting a Tag
* Select a tag from the grid to edit its label or view associated customers.
* Use the **Actions** dropdown on the grid to delete tags individually or in bulk.

![Edit or Delete Tag](/images/customer-tag/03.webp)
