# Seller End Workflow

Guide for marketplace sellers joining campaigns and managing campaign products from the seller dashboard.

### Viewing Available Campaigns

Sellers log into their Marketplace Seller Account and navigate to **Campaigns** from the dashboard menu.

![Seller Dashboard](/images/seller-dashboard.webp)

Sellers can filter campaigns by status: **Live**, **Expired**, or **Upcoming**.

![Seller Campaigns List](/images/seller-campaigns-list.webp)

---

### Joining a Campaign

1. Click **View** next to an eligible campaign.
2. Review campaign terms, discount rules, duration, and cutoff date.
3. Click **Join Campaign**.

![Seller Join Campaign](/images/seller-join-campaign.webp)

---

### Managing Campaign Products

Once joined, the seller can add products to the campaign:

![Seller Manage Products](/images/seller-manage-products.webp)

- Select products from seller catalog.
- Enable or Disable individual product participation.
- Update allocated quantities per product.
- Use mass actions to enable/disable multiple items at once.
