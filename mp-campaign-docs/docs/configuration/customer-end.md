# Customer End Experience

Guide to the storefront shopping experience for buyers participating in marketplace campaigns.

### Browsing Promotional Offers

Customers navigate to the **Offers / Campaigns** page or click on campaign promotional banners on the homepage.

![Customer Offers](/images/customer-offers.webp)

---

### Offer Details & Terms

When clicking an active campaign banner, customers can:

- Read full campaign terms & conditions.
- View countdown timers and offer validity dates.
- Share promotional links on social media platforms.

![Customer Terms & Share](/images/customer-terms-share.webp)

---

### Adding Products & Checkout

1. Browse products participating in the campaign.
2. Select desired options and click **Add to Cart**.

![Customer Add to Cart](/images/customer-add-to-cart.webp)

3. Proceed to Shopping Cart / Checkout. Campaign discounts apply automatically to eligible items.

![Customer Checkout Discount](/images/customer-checkout-discount.webp)
