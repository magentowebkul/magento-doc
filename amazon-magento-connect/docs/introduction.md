# Introduction

**Webkul Amazon Connector for Magento 2** provides store owners with a powerful solution to link their Magento 2 store with multiple Amazon Seller accounts. It facilitates synchronization between Magento 2 and Amazon marketplaces, allowing store owners to import Amazon products into Magento 2 and export Magento 2 catalog products to Amazon marketplaces. 

By automating stock levels, price updates, and importing orders, the extension streamlines multi-channel retailing and provides a single, unified interface for managing ecommerce catalog and marketplace orders.

::: tip
The extension supports connecting multiple Amazon Seller Central accounts and managing synchronization settings per account.
:::

## Key Features

- **Multi-Account Connection:** Link and manage multiple Amazon seller accounts from a single Magento backend.
- **Bi-directional Product Sync:** Export products from Magento 2 to Amazon and import products from Amazon to Magento 2.
- **Real-Time Inventory & Price Update:** Automatic background sync updates Amazon listing stock levels and prices whenever they change in Magento.
- **Centralized Order Management:** Auto-import Amazon sales orders into Magento 2, enabling centralized processing, shipment tracking, and invoice generation.
- **Flexible Attribute & Category Mapping:** Map Amazon categories and specific product attributes to their Magento 2 equivalents to ensure accurate product listings.
- **Automated Operations (Crons):** Schedule automated data sync (price, quantity, orders, and products) using Magento crontab schedules.
