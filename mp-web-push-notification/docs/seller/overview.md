# Seller Push Notification Management

Marketplace sellers can create notification templates and send push notifications directly from their seller portal dashboard.

---

## Seller Sidebar Menu

Log in to the **Seller Account Panel**. In the left navigation sidebar, click **Push Notification**:
- **Notification Templates**
- **Notification Subscribers**

![Seller Dashboard Menu](/images/image-25.png)

---

## Seller Notification Templates

Sellers can view, filter, edit, or delete their custom notification templates.

![Seller Templates Grid](/images/image-26.png)

### Creating a Seller Template
1. Click **Create Template** in the upper right corner.
2. Fill in **Title**, **Message**, **Redirect URL**, **Tags**, and upload a custom **Logo** icon.

![Seller Create Template Form](/images/image-27.png)

3. Click **Save**.

### Deleting Seller Templates
1. Select templates via checkboxes.
2. Click **Delete Template** or click the trash icon under **Action**.
3. Confirm by clicking **OK**.

![Seller Delete Template](/images/image-28.png)

---

## Seller Notification Subscribers & Sending

Sellers can view all opt-in subscribers and send targeted push alerts.

![Seller Subscribers List](/images/image-29.png)

### Dispatching Seller Push Alerts
1. Go to **Push Notification > Notification Subscribers**.
2. Select target subscribers using the checkboxes.
3. Select a template from the **Notification Template** dropdown menu.
4. Click **Send Notification**.

![Seller Send Push Notification](/images/image-30.png)

5. Click **OK** in the confirmation dialog to send the push alert.

![Seller Confirm Send](/images/image-31.png)
