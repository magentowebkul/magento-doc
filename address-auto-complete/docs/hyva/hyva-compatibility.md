# Hyvä Theme Support

For merchants running the modern **Hyvä Theme** or **Hyvä Checkout**, Webkul provides seamless compatibility through a companion package: **`Webkul_GoogleAddressAutoCompleteHyva`**.

---

## Why Use It with Hyvä?

Hyvä Theme is known for its ultra-fast page speeds and streamlined storefront design. This companion package ensures that address auto-completion works smoothly on Hyvä without sacrificing performance:

* **Instant Address Autofill:** As soon as a shopper chooses an address from Google's suggestions, all fields (Street, City, State/Province, Country, and Postal Code) fill in immediately.
* **Fast and Responsive:** Keeps page load times fast, helping your store maintain high search rankings and smooth shopping experiences.
* **Full Checkout Support:** Works on standard Hyvä address forms as well as one-page Hyvä Checkout steps and popup windows.
* **Accurate Regional Matching:** Correctly matches states, provinces, and countries in your store's dropdown menus.

---

### 3. Customer Account Address Book

Customers can also use Google address suggestions when adding or editing addresses under **My Account > Address Book**.

---

## Enabling Hyvä Support

If your store uses a Hyvä storefront:

1. Ensure the core **Google Address AutoComplete** module is installed on your store.
2. Upload the **Google Address AutoComplete Hyvä** companion folder into your store's `app/code/Webkul/` directory.
3. Once enabled, address auto-suggestions will automatically activate across your Hyvä theme.
