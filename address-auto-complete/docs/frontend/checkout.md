# Checkout Address Autocomplete

The checkout step is the most critical conversion point in any eCommerce store. **Magento 2 Google Address Auto Complete** simplifies checkout by enabling instant address autofill on both **Shipping** and **Billing** address forms for both guest visitors and registered customers.

---

## Storefront Checkout Workflow

### 1. Customer Navigates to Checkout

When shoppers add products to their shopping cart and click **Proceed to Checkout**, they arrive at the checkout page.

---

### 2. Live Street Address Suggestions

1. The customer clicks into the **Street Address** field.
2. As the customer begins typing, Google auto address suggestions appear in real time below the input.
3. The suggestion list displays complete, verified postal addresses.

![Storefront Checkout Address Autocomplete](/images/storefront_checkout_autocomplete.webp)

---

### 3. One-Click Form Auto-Population

Upon selecting an address from the suggestions:

* **Street Address:** Automatically filled with street number and street name.
* **City:** Automatically populated.
* **Country:** The corresponding country is automatically selected in the dropdown.
* **State / Province:** The appropriate region/state is automatically matched and selected.
* **Zip / Postal Code:** The verified postal code is inserted.

---

## Billing Address Autocomplete

On the payment step of the checkout:

1. When a shopper unchecks **My billing and shipping address are the same**, the billing address form appears.
2. The Google Address AutoComplete functionality operates identically on the billing form.
3. The shopper types in their billing street address and selects from the suggestions.

---

## Guest Shoppers vs Logged-In Customers

* **Guest Shoppers:** Can complete their entire address in seconds using autocomplete, reducing checkout friction.
* **Registered Customers:** When adding a new delivery destination during checkout, registered users enjoy the same autocomplete ease and can check **Save in address book** for future purchases.

---

## Mobile Friendly Experience

On smartphones and tablets, manual address typing on mobile keyboards is slow and error-prone:

* The dropdown menu provides large touch targets optimized for mobile fingertips.
* Autocomplete reduces typing between letters, numbers, and symbols.
* Faster form completion leads to higher mobile conversion rates.
