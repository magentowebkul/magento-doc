# Storefront Display & Cart

Custom options configured by marketplace vendors automatically render on storefront product pages and seamlessly integrate with shopping cart calculations and checkout summaries.

---

## Product Page Option Display

When a customer views a product on the storefront, all custom options created by the seller are displayed under the product purchasing block:

![Product Page Custom Options](/images/image-11.png)

---

## Dynamic Price Calculation

When customers select or type values into custom option fields:

1. Frontend JavaScript dynamically calculates option price adjustments (fixed dollar amounts or percentage additions).
2. The final product price updates in real-time on the storefront view without requiring a page refresh.

![Dynamic Price Update](/images/image-12.png)

---

## Shopping Cart & Checkout Summary

When a shopper adds the product to their shopping cart:

1. All selected custom options and entered values are saved with the cart item.
2. The shopping cart summary displays a clear breakdown of each option choice and price adjustment.
3. The selected options are preserved through checkout, customer order invoices, and seller order details.

![Shopping Cart Summary](/images/image-13.png)
