# Vendor Subdomain & Theme Personalization

Subscribed tenants obtain full branding independence on the marketplace platform through dedicated subdomain URLs and customizable storefront themes.

---

## Subdomain Configuration

With the **Vendor Subdomain** module active, subscribed sellers can define their tenant web address:

1. In the seller customer account, go to **Seller Profile**.
2. Locate the **Subdomain URL** configuration field.
3. Enter the desired subdomain slug (e.g., `mystore` for `mystore.yourmarketplace.com`).

![Vendor Subdomain Field](/images/image-29.png)
*Figure 10.1: Configuring the seller subdomain slug.*

4. Click **Save Profile**.

---

## Subdomain Storefront & Product Collection

Once configured, the seller's profile and catalog collection are served directly on their unique subdomain:

* **View Profile**: Directs shoppers to the dedicated subdomain storefront (e.g., `https://mystore.yourmarketplace.com`).
* **View Collection**: Opens the seller's catalog filtered exclusively to their inventory under their tenant address.

## Seller Subdomain Theme Customization

Vendors can tailor the visual aesthetic of their tenant storefront without developer intervention:

1. In the seller account sidebar, navigate to **SaaS Configuration / Theme Settings**.
2. From the **Select Theme** drop-down menu, choose an available frontend theme provided by the marketplace administrator.
3. Click **Save**.

![Seller Theme Selection](/images/image-32.png)
*Figure 10.4: Selecting a theme for the vendor subdomain.*

The selected theme is instantly applied to the vendor's subdomain storefront:
