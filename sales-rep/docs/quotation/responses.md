# Quote Responses & Cart

Representatives can formulate price quotes with custom unit pricing and tiered discounts.

## Responding to Quotes

Under the quote request, switch to the **Quote Response** tab:

![Quote Response Tab](/images/response.webp)

Edit the offered product prices or quantities:

![Edit Quote Price](/images/quote.webp)

![Submit Quote Details](/images/edit.webp)

Once submitted, the quote status changes to approved or pending customer acceptance:

![Submit Quote Request](/images/submit-quote-request.webp)

---

## Customer Checkout & Order

On the storefront, the customer receives the updated quote offer in their account:

![Customer Account Quotes](/images/Quotereq_frontend.png)

![Quote Item and Approved Price](/images/Quotereq_frontend1.png)

The customer can add the approved quote items directly to their cart at the negotiated price and complete checkout:
