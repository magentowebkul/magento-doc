# Assign Quote to Another Representative

Sales representatives or managers can reassign quote requests to colleague representatives.

## Reassigning a Quote

Open the quote request in the admin panel and select the **Assigned To** tab.

![Assigned To Tab](/images/assigned-to.webp)

Choose another representative from the available sales team members:

![Select Sales Rep](/images/sales-repo.webp)

Click **Save** or **Assign**. The quote request will be updated and assigned to the new representative:

![Assigned Confirmation](/images/assigned.webp)
