# Price List Management

Create custom price lists tailored for specific clients, tiers, or representatives via the integrated `Webkul_PriceList` dependency.

## View Price Lists

In the Magento Admin sidebar, navigate to **Price List > View Price List**.

![View Price List](/images/viewpricelist.webp)

![Price List Grid](/images/viewpl.webp)

## Price List Details

Under the price list edit view, configure general details, applicable customer groups, and linked rules:

![Price List Information](/images/pricelist-info.webp)

![Price List Rule](/images/pricelist-rule.webp)

Assign targeted customers or customer groups:

![Assign Customers](/images/pricelist-cs.webp)

![Assign Customer Groups](/images/cxgroups.webp)

::: tip Sales Rep Role Integration
Representatives granted the **Apply Pricelist** permission (`apply_pricelist`) in their user role can apply price list discounts directly when formulating quote responses for customers.
:::
