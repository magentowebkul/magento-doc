# Sales Rep Dashboard Overview

Sales representatives and administrators have access to a dedicated backend dashboard to track key metrics, customer orders, quotes, and communications.

## Accessing the Dashboard

In the Magento Admin sidebar, navigate to **Sales Rep > Dashboard**.

The dashboard provides real-time statistics including:

- **Total Sales**: Cumulative grand total revenue from completed orders mapped to the sales representative.
- **Total Discount Given**: Sum of discounts provided to customers through negotiated quote pricing.
- **Total Quotes**: Total number of primary quote requests assigned to the representative.
- **Total Orders**: Number of customer orders attributed to the representative.
- **Total Messages**: Overall count of customer messages exchanged.
- **Total Customers**: Count of distinct customers associated with the representative.
- **Recent Quotes**: Top 5 recent quote inquiries requiring review.
- **Recent Messages**: Top 5 recent customer conversation threads.
