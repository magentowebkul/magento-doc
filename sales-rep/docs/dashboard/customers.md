# Assigned Customers

Under **Sales Rep > Customers**, representatives can view and manage their dedicated customer client base.

![Assigned Customers](/images/customer.webp)

## Customer Operations

- **Search and Filter**: Filter customers by Name, Email, Account Group, or Registration Date.
- **Assign New Customer**: Link unassigned registered store customers to the sales representative.
- **Customer Profile Overview**: Access customer order history, shopping cart items, and wishlist details to tailor offers.
