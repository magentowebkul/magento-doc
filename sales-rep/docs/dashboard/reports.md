# Reports & Performance

Navigate to **Sales Rep > Reports** to view performance summaries across sales representatives.

![Reports](/images/reports.webp)

## Sales Representative Performance Grid

The reports section provides an aggregated overview of each sales representative's sales volume and customer engagement:

- **ID**: Unique representative identification number.
- **Sales Rep**: Name of the sales representative.
- **Quotes**: Total number of quote requests assigned to the representative.
- **Orders**: Total number of completed customer orders attributed to the representative.
- **Sales**: Cumulative grand total revenue generated by the representative.
- **Messages**: Total volume of customer inquiry and quotation messages exchanged.

::: tip Filtering and Sorting
Administrators can sort each metric in ascending or descending order and filter by representative name or ID to evaluate team performance.
:::
