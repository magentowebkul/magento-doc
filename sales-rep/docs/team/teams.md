# Sales Rep Teams

Assign team members to representatives and managers to enable collaborative quote management and order routing.

## Team Member Management

In Magento Admin, navigate to **Sales Rep > Sales Reps**.

![Sales Rep Team](/images/salesrepteam.webp)

## Team Member Configuration

1. Click **Edit** on a sales representative or manager user.
2. Select the **Team** tab from the left panel.
3. Check the sales representatives to be included in this user's direct team.
4. Click **Save** to apply the team associations.

::: tip Team Size Limit
The maximum number of team members that can be associated with a user is restricted by the **Max. Team Size** configuration setting in **Stores > Configuration > Webkul > Sales Rep > Managers and Team**.
:::
