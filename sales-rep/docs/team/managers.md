# Sales Rep Managers

Sales Rep Managers oversee representative performance, receive copy notifications on quote inquiries, and can reassign quotes and orders across their team members.

## Assigning Managers to Representatives

In Magento Admin, navigate to **Sales Rep > Sales Reps**.

![Sales Rep Managers](/images/sales-rep-manager.webp)

Click **Edit** on a representative or create a new representative user:

![Add New Sales Rep](/images/add-new-sales-rep.webp)

Switch to the **Managers** tab to select which manager(s) oversee this representative:

![Edit Manager Details](/images/edit-details.webp)

::: tip Manager Limit
The maximum number of managers that can be linked to a single sales representative is governed by the **Max. Number of Managers of Sales Rep** setting in **Stores > Configuration > Webkul > Sales Rep > Managers and Team**.
:::
