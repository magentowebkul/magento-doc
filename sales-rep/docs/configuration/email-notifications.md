# Email Notifications

Configure automated notifications under **Stores > Configuration > Webkul > Sales Rep**.

## Mail Settings

![Mail](/images/five.webp)

- **Default From Field**: Enter the sender email address used for outgoing transactional emails.
- **Default From Name**: Enter the sender display name used for outgoing transactional emails.

---

## Sales Rep Email Templates

![Sales Rep Emails](/images/six.webp)

Configure specialized email templates for module triggers:

- **Order Email to Sales Rep**: Sent to the assigned representative whenever an assigned customer completes an order checkout.
- **Quote Email to Admin**: Alerts store administration whenever a customer creates a new quote request.
- **Quote Email to Sales Rep**: Alerts the assigned sales representative when a customer submits a quote.
- **Quote Email to Customer**: Confirmation receipt sent to the customer after submitting a quote request.
- **Quote Email to Managers**: Notifies team managers when a team representative receives a new quote request.
- **Quote Update Email to Sales Rep**: Sent to the representative when a quote status is updated.
- **Quote Response Email to Customer**: Sent to the customer when the sales representative submits a price response or counter-offer.
- **Add to Cart Email to Customer**: Notifies the customer when their approved quote pricing is enabled for direct cart checkout.
- **Customer message Email to Sales Rep**: Notifies the representative when a customer sends an inquiry message through catalog widgets.
