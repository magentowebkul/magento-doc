# Sales Rep Settings

Configure general sales representative behavior under **Stores > Configuration > Webkul > Sales Rep > Salesrep Settings**.

![Sales rep Settings](/images/one.webp)

## Available Settings

- **Allow To use Discount while quote product is in cart**:
  - **Yes**: Permits customers to apply standard promotional coupons or discounts in the cart and checkout even when quote items with negotiated custom pricing are in the shopping cart.
  - **No**: Disables the discount field at checkout whenever an approved quote item is in the cart to avoid unintended double discounting.
