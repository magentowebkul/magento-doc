# Manage FAQ

Navigate to **AI ChatBot > ChatBot Menu > Manage FAQ** to maintain the automated knowledge base for your store.

## Adding a New FAQ

1. Click **Add New FAQ**.
2. Enter the **Question** and **Answer** content.
3. Set **Status** to `Enabled`.
4. Assign the FAQ to specific **Store Views** or select *All Store Views*.
5. Click **Save FAQ**.

## How FAQ Retrieval Works

When an FAQ is saved or updated, the extension automatically indexes the question and answer into vector embeddings. When a customer asks a matching question on the storefront, the chatbot retrieves and formats the verified answer instantly.

## Mass Actions

- **Mass Enable**: Activate multiple FAQs simultaneously.
- **Mass Disable**: Deactivate outdated FAQ entries.
- **Mass Delete**: Permanently remove selected FAQs.
