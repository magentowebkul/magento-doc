# Chat History

Navigate to **AI ChatBot > ChatBot Menu > Chat History** to monitor customer conversations in real time.

![Chat history](/images/chatbot-chat-history.png)

## Key Capabilities

- **Transcript Logs**: View entire conversation threads between customers and the AI assistant.
- **Customer Insights**: Review customer name, email address, message timestamps, and store view.
- **Filter & Search**: Quickly find specific chat sessions using keyword search or date filters.
- **Mass Delete**: Select old or inactive chat logs and perform mass deletion to maintain database efficiency.
