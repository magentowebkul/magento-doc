# Statistics Dashboard

Navigate to **AI ChatBot > ChatBot Menu > Statistics** to monitor overall chatbot performance and store interaction trends.

![Statistics](/images/chatbot-statistics.png)

## Metrics Tracked

- **Total Chats**: Total number of conversational sessions initiated by shoppers.
- **Total Queries**: Volume of individual customer questions processed.
- **Chat Reports**: Total number of issue tickets submitted to admin.
- **Manage FAQ**: Total active FAQs indexed in the knowledge base.
- **Embeddings Count**: Real-time count of indexed catalog products, CMS pages, and FAQs.
