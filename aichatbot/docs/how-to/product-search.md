# Conversational Product Search

Customers can search for products using natural language descriptions rather than exact keywords (e.g., *"Find lightweight travel bags under $50"* or *"Show me black yoga pants"*).

![Product search result](/images/chatbot-search-product-result.png)

## Features of Product Recommendations

- **Interactive Product Cards**: Displays product thumbnail image, name, price, and customer star ratings.
- **Direct Navigation**: Clicking any product card opens the product detail page in the store.
- **Semantic Understanding**: Uses vector similarity embeddings to match intent even when exact keywords differ.
